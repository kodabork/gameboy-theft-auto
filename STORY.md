# GTA (Gameboy Theft Auto): Steel Harbor — story bible (v0.26)

The plan for a story of about **6 hours**: 4 acts, 40 story missions, plus side content. Each act opens a new district of the bigger map (see `ROADMAP.md`).

**Act One is in the game now**: the 9 existing jobs, rewritten for Niko. Acts Two to Four are the plan: their missions get built once the map grows.

## Premise
**Niko B** grew up on the Steel Harbor waterfront, the son of a tugboat captain. At 18 he enlisted in the army and spent eight years deployed overseas, rarely writing home. He came out a sergeant, with steady nerves, a soldier's eye for trouble and no plan for civilian life. He comes home for one reason: his father **Sal** is dead, lost when his tug, the *Lucky Marie*, sank in a storm. The police called it an accident.

At the funeral, loan shark **Victor Moran** turns up holding a page from his ledger. It says Sal owed him $10,000, and that the debt passed to his son, with the family house as collateral. Niko's younger sister **Ana**, a night-shift nurse, still lives in that house.

Niko knows his father never borrowed a dime. But Moran owns half the docks and has a detective on his payroll, so Niko starts paying.

Theme: **debts, who invents them, and who pays.** Steel Harbor runs on favours, and Niko learns to trade them.

## Side content (about 1-2 hours)
- **Vehicle jobs:**
  - Taxi fares (taxi).
  - Ambulance runs for Ana's hospital (ambulance).
  - Police vigilante (police car).
  - Marta's car export lists (steal specific cars).
- **Tug jobs with Rudy** (after Act Two): tow barges, boat races around the harbor.
- **Hidden packages:** Sal's 50 lost tug parts around the city. Rewards come at milestones (weapons at home, armour, and the restored *Lucky Marie* at 50).
- **Stunt jumps**, a **stats screen**, and properties to buy (Marta's garage share, the club, a dock warehouse) that pay a little income.
- **Kings turf fights** in Act Two and later: random Danny Moran crews attack Kings corners.

## How missions are built
- Act One uses the existing objective types: steal and deliver, collect and deliver, kill target(s), destroy cars, timed drive, chase until bail-out, lose the wanted level.
- Acts Two to Four also need:
  - **tail** (stay within range but not too close)
  - **spot/photograph** (stand at points)
  - **defend** (waves at a location)
  - **escort** (an NPC in your car or on foot)
  - **boat chase**
  - **boss** (one armoured enemy)
- **Cutscenes:** in-engine, with letterbox bars and a dialogue box. The mission giver stands in the street facing Niko: an establishing two-shot first, then over-the-shoulder cuts to whoever is speaking. Afterwards the giver walks away. Portraits stay off unless the owner supplies art.
- **New people needed:**
  - **Female ped models** for Ana and Dee. Marta and Jinx's outfits could also get female versions. Each new set is about 1 MB of ROM with near and far frames.
  - A Blackline guard (a recoloured cop).

## Systems
- **Title screen:** a "GTA" wordmark with NEW GAME / CONTINUE.
- **Pause menu (START):** resume, save, radio, quit to title.
- **Saving:** battery SRAM, plus an autosave after every job. You can't save during a job or while wanted. Loading puts you back in the car you saved in, or on foot with no car.
- **A crash alone never kills the player**, but a car that blows up with you inside does (WASTED). A burning car gives you at least 4 s and you can bail out at any speed.
- **Car damage, from 0 to 1000:** smokes from 650, burns from 880 and then explodes. Gunfire, rams and blasts all count.
- **Repairs:** Marta's garage charges $100, or $300 if the car is burning.
